Shopify product section

Variant & availability checks

A buyer choosing a different finish should see its price, image, purchase limits and availability update together. This sample runs the Studio Aster product section against a small, fictional catalog.

What passed

LiquidJS 10.21.1 rendered nine catalog cases. Twelve rendering checks passed. Chrome passed five interaction scenarios at both 390 px and 1200 px.

  • Variant pricing. Cobalt displayed $78. Chalk displayed $92 and changed the photo. Its quantity started at two, rejected three and accepted four. The intercepted form contained variant 102 and quantity 4.
  • Sold-out selection. Archive displayed $65 with a disabled purchase button. Selecting an available variant restored the action and removed the prior quantity maximum.
  • Missing images. Moving from a photo-free variant to Chalk showed its image. Switching back removed the stale photo and alt text.
  • Catalog limits. The sample rendered 50 variant options. The 51-variant and subscription fixtures showed the unsupported-product notice. The empty editor fixture displayed a placeholder and omitted the purchase form.

Two observed states

Try the rendered product section

Choose Chalk and enter four. “Add to cart” displays the variant and quantity locally. This sample cannot create an order.

Acceptance in a merchant theme

The fixture supplies product data, USD formatting, local photo URLs and an intercepted form. Final acceptance in an authorized, unpublished Shopify theme covers the native cart response, quantities actually accepted by inventory, theme cart drawer behavior, app blocks and the merchant’s market-specific pricing. Shopify checkout and payments have not been tested.
